Asya Ünal

Computer Science · Bilkent University

I’m a final-year computer science student and undergraduate researcher at Bilkent University. I work on robot learning, generative models, and multimodal AI, with an interest in how intelligent systems perceive, plan, and act.

My research spans diffusion policies at LiRA Lab, adaptive replanning with Prof. A. Yavuz Oruç at Maryland, and semantic fidelity in super-resolution with Prof. A. Murat Tekalp at KUIS AI. At CodeAPeel, I contribute to desktop and web platforms for teaching computer architecture, programming, and mathematics.

Before computer science, I studied medicine at Ankara University for three years and held a TÜBİTAK STAR research internship at Hacettepe University. I’m applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
