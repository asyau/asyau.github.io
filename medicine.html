Medicine × computation

From medical training to models of minds and health.

I am a computer scientist with three years of medical training, interested in computational neuroscience, neural representation learning, and clinically grounded AI.

Discuss research

The bridge

I began at Ankara University School of Medicine, where anatomy, physiology, and clinical observation taught me to think about biological systems as systems: adaptive, noisy, and inseparable from context. A research internship at Hacettepe University's Institute of Neurological Sciences brought that interest into the lab through work on cerebral and retinal microcirculation.

I later moved to computer science to gain the tools to model complex systems directly. Since then, I have built transformer and agentic-AI systems, worked across computer vision and time-series learning, and co-authored three peer-reviewed papers. My goal is not to place medicine and computing side by side. It is to work where each makes the other more rigorous.

Research objective

I am preparing for research and PhD work on fundamental brain science and trustworthy health AI—especially projects where strong machine-learning engineering can unlock better scientific questions, not merely higher benchmark scores.

Seeking research collaborations and Fall 2027 PhD opportunities in computational neuroscience, neuro-AI, and biomedical data science.

Questions I want to work on

Machine learning as a scientific instrument.

The common thread is not a specific model family. It is using computation to make biological and clinical questions more precise.

01

Neural representations

How can sequence and foundation models learn representations that generalize across neurons, subjects, sessions, and recording modalities?

02

Neural dynamics

How can latent-variable and dynamical models recover computation from noisy population recordings without mistaking pipeline artifacts for biology?

03

Connectomics

How can measured wiring and large-scale imaging constrain models of circuit function, learning, and behavior?

04

Language, grounding & cognition

What do language models reveal—and fail to reveal—about meaning, reasoning, and biologically grounded intelligence?

05

Trustworthy clinical AI

How should clinical models be evaluated when documentation, labels, workflows, and patient needs are all imperfect proxies?

06

Multimodal health systems

How can imaging, signals, text, and longitudinal context be combined without erasing the uncertainty of each modality?
