Research

I’m interested in how learning systems perceive, plan, and act—and how we evaluate whether their outputs are grounded in evidence.

Robot learning · Diffusion and flow-matching policies · Compute-aware planning · VLM-based evaluation of generative models

Ongoing work

  • Adaptive replanning for robot policies

    Oct 2026 — present

    University of Maryland · Prof. A. Yavuz Oruç

    Undergraduate Researcher

    Investigating when a frozen vision-language-action policy should replan, and whether adaptive replanning can outperform fixed schedules at equal compute.

    Working with SmolVLA on MetaWorld and LIBERO, with provenance tracking, exact replay, counterfactual rollouts, and explicit query-cost accounting.

  • Diffusion policies and trajectory planning

    Sep 2026 — present

    LiRA Lab, Bilkent University · Asst. Prof. Özgür S. Öğüz

    Undergraduate Researcher

    Developing a compute-aware adaptive replanning rule for diffusion and flow-matching robot policies: a single probe call estimates the KL divergence between the current plan and what the policy would now choose, using one budget to decide both when to replan and how much of the prior plan to reuse.

    Running controlled push-perturbation experiments on Push-T against fixed-window flow and diffusion-policy baselines, targeting ICML 2027.

  • Semantic fidelity in diffusion super-resolution

    Aug 2026 — present

    KUIS AI Center, Koç University · Prof. A. Murat Tekalp

    Undergraduate Researcher

    Studying whether reconstructed scene text is supported by the low-resolution input, using OCR and vision-language models.

    Fine-tuned Qwen2.5-VL-3B with LoRA as a reference-free judge of reconstruction candidates, keeping AI-curated supervision separate from human judgments.

    Built reproducible training and evaluation pipelines with resumable checkpoints, source-grouped splits, and separate strict and diagnostic scoring.

    Used the fine-tuned judge to guide the diffusion generator’s output selection: scoring 100-candidate pools per source and selecting the candidates judged most semantically faithful to the low-resolution input.

Earlier research

  • Industrial visual inspection and anomaly detection

    Aug 2024 — Aug 2026

    Teknopar Industrial Automation

    Machine Learning Researcher

    Owned industrial visual inspection end to end, from CNN/YOLO defect detection to deployment in a production cobot inspection cell. This work produced three papers: first-author at IEEE FiCloud 2025 and MobiWIS 2026, and co-authored Real-Time Anomaly Detection in Industry 4.0 Using Asset Administration Shell (Springer LNCS, MobiWIS 2025).

    Production evaluation cut per-unit quality-check time from 82 s to 61 s and operator viewing time by 82%, under EU projects AI-PRISM (Horizon Europe) and PREDISURF (Eurostars).

    Benchmarked deep learning architectures for optical inspection and fine-tuned DistilBERT on imbalanced industrial error logs.
